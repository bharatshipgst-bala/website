Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

ShipGST India ("the App") helps Shopify merchants in India book courier shipments and generate GST-compliant invoices. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Merchant information: store name, Shopify access token, GSTIN, business address, pickup warehouse pincode, and courier (Delhivery / Blue Dart) API keys you provide.
  • Order information: order details from your Shopify store needed to generate invoices and shipments, including customer name, shipping address, phone number, line items, HSN codes and prices.
  • Shipment information: AWB numbers, tracking IDs and label files.

How we use information

  • To calculate GST (CGST/SGST/IGST) and generate tax invoices.
  • To check pincode serviceability, book shipments and generate shipping labels with your chosen courier.
  • To sync tracking information back to your Shopify store.

Sharing

Order and address data is shared only with the courier partners you connect (Delhivery, Blue Dart) as required to deliver shipments. We do not sell personal information.

Data retention & deletion

Data is retained while the App is installed. When you uninstall, we delete your store data in accordance with Shopify's mandatory GDPR webhooks (customer data request, customer redact, shop redact).

Security

Access tokens and API keys are stored encrypted, and all traffic is served over HTTPS.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@example.com.
